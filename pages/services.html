What Won't I Do?

A plumbing, electrical, or structural engineering license

A vehicle other than a Prius hatchback (While she can haul a surprising amount and vareity of stuff, some things just need to be hauled *outside* the vehicle and her towing capacity is unsurprisingly, uh, none haha)

Moving very large or heavy items (I'm handy but I am also not designed to have a large towing capacity :P)

Specialised tools that I don't currently have and wouldn't be able to buy or rent for the job

If you're not sure whether any of these apply to what you need a hand with, just ask. I'll let you know if I can help and, if I can't, I'd be happy to ask around for you to see who could.
