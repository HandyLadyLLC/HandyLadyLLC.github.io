A helping hand, built on trust.

Handy Lady LLC is a one-woman handyperson business serving St. Petersburg, FL and the surrounding area. The goal is simple: show up, do good work, and charge a fair, predictable rate — no estimates, no upsells, no surprises on the bill.

I'm here to empower and support female, LGBTQ+, neurodivergent, and retired clients and people with alternative lifestyles and interests. Every job is treated with professionalism and without judgment — unusual requests are welcome, as long as they're legal and non-sexual in nature.

Meet Betty

A sweet rescued mini Australian shepherd with a bit of separation anxiety, Betty is happy to tag along to job sites as a well-behaved shadow, anywhere dogs are welcome. Just say the word.

How Jobs Are Scoped

Before any visit, we'll talk through exactly what's needed so the right materials come along on the first trip. No formal estimate process — just a conversation and a clear hourly rate.

What's Off the Table

Licensed structural, plumbing, and electrical work isn't offered, and nothing illegal or sexual will be entertained as part of a job — that keeps things safe and professional for everyone.

FAQ

Good questions.

Do you give estimates or quotes?

Nope. If you want a total to plan around, a time limit can be set for each visit before the job starts. Check out the Rates page for more info.

What area do you serve?

Anywhere in the Pinellas County given enough availability. Closer to central St. Pete when time is limited. Jobs farther away may be possible if scheduled in advance. Select "Need a Hand?" on any page if you'd like to see what's possible.

How do I pay?

Check out my Rates page for payment options.

Do materials cost extra?

You're charged exactly what the materials cost, with no markup. We'll talk through what's needed before the visit, and time spent gathering materials beforehand isn't billed. If a supply run is needed mid-job, it's on the clock but you'll know what's being picked up and from where first.

What jobs won't you do?

Check out the list at the bottom of my Services page.

Are there any discounts?

Yes — $10 off for leaving me a review on Google, $10 off for giving me permission to post before-and-after photos of your project in my gallery, and $20 off a future visit for every referral who books a job with me.

What are your hours?

Visit the Schedule page to see my availability. Select "Need a Hand?" at the top of any page to see about scheduling a job. .

Does your dog tag along?

If you're a fan of four-legged friends, Betty — a well-behaved rescued mini Australian shepherd — is happy to tag along wherever dogs are welcome. Just mention it when you reach out.

Is anything off-limits to ask about?

Unusual requests are genuinely welcome, as long as they're legal and respectful. While sex work is valid, massges are fantastic, and spankings can be fun, those are not services that I offer.
