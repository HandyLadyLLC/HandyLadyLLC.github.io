Why people hire Handy Lady LLC

Transparent Pricing & Communication

Flat rates, direct costs, and clear communication before, during, and after every job. No estimates. No markups. No guessing games.

Respect & Reliability

You can count on me to be chill and professional from start to finish. No ghosting, volatility, throwing shade, or leaving a mess.

Inclusion, Empowerment, & Support

If you've ever felt overlooked, dismissed, or unsupported — by a contractor, a landlord, or anywhere else — that's not what happens here. One of my biggest goals is making sure women, LGBTQ+, neurodivergent, and retired clients get the respect, acceptance, and support they should have had all along.

Peace of Mind

You can rest at ease with me on the job. If you're away from home, you can get check-ins and photo updates of your place, pets, plants, or whatever else you need cared for or checked on while you're away. If you or your pets simply feel more at ease with a woman on the job, you've come to the right place.

You Do You

Into ENM, naturalism, kink, BDSM, nude art, fandom, cosplay, etc. and don't want to feel like you have to hide who you are or what you love when you have visitors? As long as it's safe, legal, and there's consent all around, I'm your gal. No side-eyes or comments from me and I won't talk or post about it after unless you ask me to showcase your project in my gallery.

I'm up for a challenge, weird or not

Need help with an unusual project and have no idea who to ask? Ask me! Chances are if I think I can manage it safely, legally, and competently, I'd willing to give it a chance if you're willing to give me one. If it turns out I'm not able to swing it, you'd only pay for the materials (not the time spent on the attempt) and I'll do my best to help you find someone who can make it happen.

No Judgment

Living with a chronic condition, a disability, depression, hoarding tendencies, a phobia, etc. and worried about what visitors to your home might think or say? No worries needed; I'm here to help. No judgment. Full confidentiality.
