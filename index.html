Why people hire Handy Lady LLC

Transparent Pricing

Flat rates, direct costs, and clear communication before, during, and after every job. No estimates. No markups. No guessing games.

Respect & Reliability

You can count on clear communication from the first message to the final walkthrough — what's happening, when, and what it costs, with no vanishing acts, rude remarks, or mess left behind.

Inclusion, Empowerment, & Support

If you've ever felt overlooked, dismissed, or unsupported — by a contractor, a landlord, or anywhere else — that's not what happens here. One of my biggest goals is making sure women, LGBTQ+, neurodivergent, and retired clients get the respect, acceptance, and support they should have had all along.

Peace of Mind

You can rest at ease with me on the job. If you're away from home, you can get check-ins and photo updates of your place, pets, plants, or whatever else you need cared for or checked on while you're away. If you or your pets simply feel more at ease with a woman on the job, you've come to the right place.

You Do You

Into ENM, naturalism, kink/BDSM, nude art, fandom, cosplay, or anything else that may have made you hesitate to hire someone to come into your home in the past or need help with an unusual project (installing a pole dancing setup, storage for an epic toy collection, a rolling library ladder, a secret bookcase door, etc.)? As long as it's safe, legal, and there's consent, I'm cool with it; No side-eyes or comments from me and I won't talk or post about it after unless you ask me to showcase your project in my gallery.

No Judgment

Living with a chronic condition, a disability, depression, hoarding tendencies, medical equipment, or a phobia or two? None of that needs an explanation here — it's just part of being human, and it won't change how I treat you or your home. No judgment. Full confidentiality.
